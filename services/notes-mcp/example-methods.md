level: line
check: Every sentence earns its length; no throat-clearing, no stacked qualifiers.

Rewrite the passage at about two thirds of its length. Keep every claim and every number.

## Animate the background

level: line
check: Settings and supporting facts move - verbs, not inventories.

Give the background detail motion: turn lists of facts into things happening.
