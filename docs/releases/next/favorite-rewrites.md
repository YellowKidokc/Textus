### New

- Select a sentence, several sentences or paragraphs and choose a favorite
  rewrite action. Academic phrasing and shortening are included; edit, reorder
  or add your own actions without a 20- or 30-action limit.
- Compare each passage's original and generated versions in place with left
  and right controls. Each pending passage has its own outline and Keep or
  Reject controls; keeping one leaves the others available to review.
- Rewrite history keeps the original, every requested version, instructions,
  provider and model, and the version you kept or discarded. Rejecting a
  rewrite does not delete it. Undo and redo update the recorded outcome.
  Print the report or export a standalone HTML report or the complete JSON
  record. History is stored locally and is not trimmed to recent versions.
